# Pause

A modern app for an ancient practice.
Put your phone down one day a week.
Do it with your family and friends.

**Your people still get through. Instagram doesn’t.**

There is no paywall. Every feature is in the app.

## What’s inside

- Cinematic onboarding: sacred day → who still reaches you → who you’re with → how you’ll keep the day
- Saturday or Sunday, sundown to sundown, **starts on its own**
- Lifelines: calls, messages, Slack DMs/@mentions, Maps
- VIP people who still ring (Mom, partner, kid, school, work)
- Quiet apps: Instagram, TikTok, X, YouTube, and the rest
- Circle + household rest + invite by name
- Optional: circle must approve a pause
- Intention for the day (*to eat slowly*, *to be present*…)
- Live rest screen with quiet prompts
- Demo of the deal: Instagram stays quiet, Mom can still call
- Slide to pause with a reason your circle can see
- Postcard recap + a note to yourself
- Weekday blocks (dinner, deep work, wind down, sleep)
- Deep rest timer
- Candle app icon, dusk skies, dinner-table stills

Blocking is simulated in this prototype (no Screen Time API yet). The product is fully usable as a ritual, a circle, and a setup for real blocking later.
